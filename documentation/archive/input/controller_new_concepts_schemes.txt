SCHEME 1: AUTISYMMETRY (BILATERAL FLICK)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ABSTRACT:
A discrete, rhythm-driven layout engineered around speedcubing hand independence 
and algorithmic finger tricks. The scheme treats both thumbs as equal partners: 
vertical thumb flicks control the respective outer flanks (R on the right hand, 
L on the left hand), while horizontal flicks across EITHER stick share the top 
layer (U). This ambidextrous top-layer channel allows solvers to alternate left 
and right thumb flicks at high speed during standard CFOP triggers (e.g., R U R' U'). 
Bumper clutches shift the stick planes into secondary depth tiers (F, B, D) and 
full-frame axial pitch/yaw without taking thumbs off the sticks.

SYSTEM BEHAVIOR & GATING:
• Flick Threshold: Magnitude >= 0.65 triggers a 90° face turn instant pop.
• Reset Threshold: Magnitude must return to < 0.20 before another turn can fire.
• Layer Turn Mode: Discrete instantaneous 90° increments.

MAPPINGS & COMBOS:
• Primary Outer Layer Mode (Neutral / Tier 0 - No Bumpers Held)
    - Right Stick Y-Axis Up: Move R' (Right layer counter-clockwise)
    - Right Stick Y-Axis Down: Move R (Right layer clockwise)
    - Left Stick Y-Axis Up: Move L (Left layer clockwise)
    - Left Stick Y-Axis Down: Move L' (Left layer counter-clockwise)
    - Right Stick X-Axis Left: Move U (Top layer clockwise)
    - Right Stick X-Axis Right: Move U' (Top layer counter-clockwise)
    - Left Stick X-Axis Left: Move U (Top layer clockwise - mirrored channel)
    - Left Stick X-Axis Right: Move U' (Top layer counter-clockwise - mirrored channel)

• Secondary Depth & Base Mode (Tier 1 - Hold Either L1 OR R1)
    - Right Stick Y-Axis Up: Move F' (Front layer counter-clockwise)
    - Right Stick Y-Axis Down: Move F (Front layer clockwise)
    - Left Stick Y-Axis Up: Move B (Back layer clockwise)
    - Left Stick Y-Axis Down: Move B' (Back layer counter-clockwise)
    - Right Stick X-Axis Left: Move D (Bottom layer clockwise)
    - Right Stick X-Axis Right: Move D' (Bottom layer counter-clockwise)
    - Left Stick X-Axis Left: Move D (Bottom layer clockwise - mirrored channel)
    - Left Stick X-Axis Right: Move D' (Bottom layer counter-clockwise - mirrored channel)

• Axial Reorientation Mode (Tier 2 - Squeeze BOTH L1 + R1)
    - Either Stick Y-Axis Up: Whole-Cube Pitch Forward (x')
    - Either Stick Y-Axis Down: Whole-Cube Pitch Backward (x)
    - Either Stick X-Axis Left: Whole-Cube Yaw Left (y')
    - Either Stick X-Axis Right: Whole-Cube Yaw Right (y)

• Core Slice Deck (D-Pad Direct Taps - No Modifiers)
    - D-Pad Up: Slice M' (Upward center vertical cut)
    - D-Pad Down: Slice M (Downward center vertical cut)
    - D-Pad Left: Slice E (Equator slice rotating left)
    - D-Pad Right: Slice E' (Equator slice rotating right)

• Wide Layer Clutch (Hold Left Trigger L2)
    - Hold L2 + Any Face Action: Turns layer 2-slices deep (Wide: r, l, u, d, f, b)

• Free Look Camera Mode (Hold Right Trigger R2)
    - Right Stick: Free Orbit (Camera pitch and yaw around cube)
    - Left Stick: Camera Pan / Dolly Zoom
    - Release R2: Smooth snap back to locked isometric solve perspective

SCHEME 2: BACK SIDE 2 PI (ANALOG RELATIVE CRANK)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ABSTRACT:
A continuous rotational model that treats the thumbstick perimeter as a 1:1 
mechanical crank handle. Solvers use dedicated shoulder inputs to clamp a 
target face, then stir the right stick along the rim to rotate that slice. It 
employs an "Enter-as-Origin" floating zero: the angle where the thumbstick breaches 
the deadzone is assigned 0°, ensuring slice rotation tracks smoothly without 
positional snapping upon entry. Releasing the stick into deadzone automatically 
quantizes the slice to the nearest 90° increment with haptic/audio detents.

SYSTEM BEHAVIOR & GATING:
• Engage Radius: Magnitude >= 0.45 sets Origin Angle and engages 1:1 tracking.
• Release Radius: Magnitude < 0.25 disengages, snaps to nearest 90°, and bakes.
• Detents: Audio tick and haptic pulse fire at every ±90° (π/2 radian) interval.

MAPPINGS & COMBOS:
• Face Selection Clutches (Shoulder Clamping)
    - Hold R1 + Sweep Right Stick: Rotate Right Face (R) 1:1 with stick angle
    - Hold L1 + Sweep Right Stick: Rotate Left Face (L) 1:1 with stick angle
    - Hold R2 + Sweep Right Stick: Rotate Front Face (F) 1:1 with stick angle
    - Hold L2 + Sweep Right Stick: Rotate Back Face (B) 1:1 with stick angle
    - Squeeze L1 + R1 + Sweep Right Stick: Rotate Top Face (U) 1:1 with stick angle
    - Squeeze L2 + R2 + Sweep Right Stick: Rotate Bottom Face (D) 1:1 with stick angle

• Core Slice Deck (D-Pad Direct Taps - No Modifiers)
    - D-Pad Up: Slice M' (Upward center vertical cut)
    - D-Pad Down: Slice M (Downward center vertical cut)
    - D-Pad Left: Slice E (Equator slice rotating left)
    - D-Pad Right: Slice E' (Equator slice rotating right)

• Axial Reorientation Mode (Hold R3 Stick Click)
    - Left Stick Y-Axis Up / Down: Whole-Cube Pitch (x' / x)
    - Left Stick X-Axis Left / Right: Whole-Cube Yaw (y' / y)

• Free Look Camera Inspection (Neutral Sticks - No Clutches Held)
    - Left Stick (Axes 0, 1): Camera Pan / Zoom
    - Right Stick (Axes 2, 3): Camera Orbit (Pitch / Yaw)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCHEME 3: STICK FIGURES (SPATIAL INDY GRAB - AMBIDEXTROUS CLAMP & DRAG)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ABSTRACT:
A fully ambidextrous, spatial-targeting layout that eliminates bumper modifiers 
for face selection. The scheme maps all six faces directly to the physical 
vectors of both sticks: pushing outward targets the outer flanks (L on left stick, 
R on right stick), pushing inward reaches into depth (F on left stick, B on right 
stick), while vertical tilts (U and D) are mirrored symmetrically across both sticks. 
The system is explicitly ambidextrous: EITHER stick can initiate and clamp a face, 
and manipulation can be executed either solo (sweeping the same thumb along the rim) 
or bilaterally (holding the clamp with one hand while cranking with the other).

SYSTEM BEHAVIOR & GATING:
• Spatial Clamp Threshold: Magnitude >= 0.45 latches target slice.
• Execution Toggles (Sandbox Settings):
    - Solo Drag: Sweeping the clamping stick along the rim drives rotation.
    - Vise & Crank: Clamping stick holds target; opposite stick stirs to crank.
• Commit Triggers (Sandbox Settings):
    - Release Clamp: Snaps and commits the moment the targeting stick resets.
    - Release Crank: Snaps and commits when the driving stick returns to center.

MAPPINGS & COMBOS:
• Ambidextrous Left-Stick Target Map (Left Flank + Front)
    - Push Left (Outward): Target Left Layer (L)
    - Push Right (Inward): Target Front Layer (F)
    - Push Up: Target Top Layer (U) [Shared bilateral target]
    - Push Down: Target Bottom Layer (D) [Shared bilateral target]

• Ambidextrous Right-Stick Target Map (Right Flank + Back)
    - Push Right (Outward): Target Right Layer (R)
    - Push Left (Inward): Target Back Layer (B)
    - Push Up: Target Top Layer (U) [Shared bilateral target]
    - Push Down: Target Bottom Layer (D) [Shared bilateral target]

• Dual-Stick Vise Execution Roles
    - Left Hand Clamp + Right Stick Crank: Left selects, Right drives rotation
    - Right Hand Clamp + Left Stick Crank: Right selects, Left drives rotation

• Core Slice Deck (D-Pad Direct Taps - No Modifiers)
    - D-Pad Up: Slice M' (Upward center vertical cut)
    - D-Pad Down: Slice M (Downward center vertical cut)
    - D-Pad Left: Slice E (Equator slice rotating left)
    - D-Pad Right: Slice E' (Equator slice rotating right)

• Double Mega Slice (Squeeze Dual Bumpers L1 + R1)
    - Hold L1 + R1 + Any Clamped Face: Turns layer 2-slices deep (Wide: f, u, r, l, d, b)

• Whole-Frame Reorientation Mode (Hold R3 Stick Click)
    - Left Stick Y-Axis Up / Down: Whole-Cube Pitch (x' / x)
    - Left Stick X-Axis Left / Right: Whole-Cube Yaw (y' / y)

• Free Look Camera Mode (Hold Either Trigger L2 OR R2)
    - Right Stick: Camera Orbit (Pitch / Yaw with inverted Y option)
    - Left Stick: Camera Pan / Dolly Zoom
    - Release Trigger: Springs camera back to locked isometric solve angle


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCHEME 4: STICKY ICKY (POINTER & CRANK / VISE GRIP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ABSTRACT:
A strict separation-of-concerns architecture designed for maximum physical 
ergonomics. The left thumb serves exclusively as a stationary targeting vise, 
while the right thumb acts as a universal rotational motor. The left stick rests 
in neutral on the Front (F) face to eliminate stick fatigue during forward turns, 
cardinal tilts isolate U, D, L, and R, and an L3 stick click plunges through the 
core to lock Back (B). Holding both bumpers engages wide double-slices, holding 
either trigger unlocks free-look inspection, and R3 allows full-frame reorientation.

SYSTEM BEHAVIOR & GATING:
• Left Stick Targeting: Continuous directional clamping.
• Right Stick Driver: Enter-as-Origin relative crank (Radius >= 0.45 engages).
• Auto-Snap: Releasing right stick into deadzone (< 0.25) bakes move to matrix.
• Detents: Audio and haptic pulse fire at every 90° increment.

MAPPINGS & COMBOS:
• Left Stick Face Targeting (The Vise)
    - Neutral Center (Deadzone): Target Front Face (F) [Zero-tension default]
    - Tilt Up: Target Top Layer (U)
    - Tilt Down: Target Bottom Layer (D)
    - Tilt Left: Target Left Face (L)
    - Tilt Right: Target Right Face (R)
    - Press / Hold L3 (Stick Click): Target Back Face (B) [Plunge into depth]

• Right Stick Rotational Driver (The Wrench)
    - Sweep Perimeter Clockwise: Rotates active targeted layer clockwise
    - Sweep Perimeter Counter-Clockwise: Rotates targeted layer counter-clockwise
    - Return to Center: Snaps to nearest 90° and bakes move to permutation matrix

• Double Mega Slice (Squeeze Dual Bumpers L1 + R1)
    - Hold L1 + R1 + Left Stick Target + Sweep Right Stick: 
      Turns layer 2-slices deep (Wide turns: f, u, r, l, d, b)

• Whole-Frame Reorientation Mode (Hold R3 Stick Click)
    - Left Stick Y-Axis Up: Whole-Cube Pitch Forward (x')
    - Left Stick Y-Axis Down: Whole-Cube Pitch Backward (x)
    - Left Stick X-Axis Left: Whole-Cube Yaw Left (y')
    - Left Stick X-Axis Right: Whole-Cube Yaw Right (y)

• Core Slice Deck (D-Pad Direct Taps - No Modifiers)
    - D-Pad Up: Slice M' (Upward center vertical cut)
    - D-Pad Down: Slice M (Downward center vertical cut)
    - D-Pad Left: Slice E (Equator slice rotating left)
    - D-Pad Right: Slice E' (Equator slice rotating right)

• Free Look Camera Mode (Hold Either Trigger L2 OR R2)
    - Trigger Held: Mutes all puzzle slice interactions
    - Right Stick (Axes 2, 3): Camera Orbit around origin (Pitch / Yaw)
    - Left Stick (Axes 0, 1): Camera Pan (Horizontal) and Dolly Zoom (Vertical)
    - Release Trigger: Smoothly snaps camera back to locked solve view
