### The Three Control Schemes

**1. Autisymmetry (Bilateral Flick)**
Designed around speedcubing muscle memory and hand independence. Outer layers sit directly on neutral thumbsticks: vertical swipes handle the outer wings ($R$ on the right stick, $L$ on the left), while the horizontal axis on *either* stick turns the top layer ($U$), allowing alternating hand rhythms for standard triggers. Holding either bumper shifts the sticks down a tier to depth and bottom layers ($F$ on right stick, $B$ on left, $D$ on either horizontal axis), while clamping both bumpers locks the cube into axial pitch ($x$) and yaw ($y$) reorientation.

**2. Back Side 2 pi (crank)**
Treats the controller like an analog crank. You hold a bumper or trigger to clamp an active face, then stir the thumbstick along the outer rim. Using an "Enter-as-Origin" floating zero, the exact angle where the stick breaches the deadzone becomes $0^\circ$, so the face rotates $1:1$ without jumping on entry. Releasing the stick into the center deadzone quantizes the turn to the nearest $90^\circ$ increment.

**3. Stick Figures (Directional Clamping & Drag)**
Eliminates bumper modifiers by using the natural geometry of both thumbsticks to map all six faces. Pushing outward selects exterior wings ($L$ on left stick, $R$ on right stick), pushing upward or downward selects $U$ or $D$ on either hand, and pushing inward into the dead space selects depth ($F$ on left stick, $B$ on right stick). Once clamped, the slice can either be dragged along the rim by the same thumb or cranked with the opposite hand like a vise.

### Logic Flow & Sandbox State Machine

```text
[Input Event Received]
  │
  ├──► SCHEME: Autisymmetry
  │      ├── Both Bumpers Held (L1 + R1)?
  │      │     ├─ Yes ──► Axis 1/3 (Y) -> Pitch (x/x') | Axis 0/2 (X) -> Yaw (y/y')
  │      │     └─ No
  │      ├── Either Bumper Held (L1 or R1)?
  │      │     ├─ Yes (Tier 1) ──► R-Y: F/F' | L-Y: B/B' | Shared X: D/D'
  │      │     └─ No  (Tier 0) ──► R-Y: R/R' | L-Y: L/L' | Shared X: U/U'
  │      └── Magnitude > flick_threshold -> Trigger Move -> Require < reset_threshold
  │
  ├──► SCHEME: Back Side 2Pi
  │      ├── Face Modifier Held? (L1=L, R1=R, L2=B, R2=F, Both=U)
  │      │     ├─ No  ──► Idle / Camera Pass-through
  │      │     └─ Yes ──► Active Slice Latched
  │      ├── Stick Radius >= engage_radius (0.45)?
  │      │     ├── First Frame ──► Set origin_angle = current_stick_angle (Delta = 0)
  │      │     └── Tracking    ──► Rotate active slice 1:1 with (current - origin)
  │      └── Stick Radius < release_radius (0.25)?
  │            └── Snap accumulated delta to nearest 90° -> Commit Permutation
  │
  └──► SCHEME: Stick Figures
		 ├── Stick A tilted beyond engage_radius (0.45)?
		 │     ├── L-Stick: Up=U, Down=D, Left(Out)=L, Right(In)=F
		 │     └── R-Stick: Up=U, Down=D, Right(Out)=R, Left(In)=B
		 ├── Evaluation Mode:
		 │     ├── Toggle: "solo_drag"       ──► Sweep Stick A along rim -> Snap on release
		 │     └── Toggle: "vise_and_crank"  ──► Hold Stick A (clamp) + Stir Stick B (crank)
		 └── Commit Trigger:
			   ├── Toggle: "release_crank"   ──► Snap when crank returns to deadzone
			   └── Toggle: "release_clamp"   ──► Snap immediately when Stick A is released

```
